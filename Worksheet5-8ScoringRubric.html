Worksheet 5.8 — Scoring Rubric

This page explains how your work will be graded on the trigonometry problem-solving worksheet. Correct answers matter, but clear mathematical communication also matters.

Important: A correct final answer with little or no supporting work will not receive full credit. You are expected to show organized reasoning, label quantities clearly, and include units in your final answers when appropriate.

General Expectations for All Problems

Required
  • Show enough work so that another student could follow your reasoning.
  • Label important sides, angles, distances, and variables.
  • Include units in final answers when the problem involves measurement.
  • Use a diagram when the problem calls for geometric interpretation.
Possible Deductions
  • Missing units: minus 1 point
  • No diagram when appropriate: minus 1 point
  • Unlabeled variables or quantities: minus 1 point
  • Disorganized scratch work submitted as final work: up to minus 2 points
Multiple Choice Policy
  • Even on multiple choice, you must show setup and supporting work.
  • No work means very limited credit, even if the choice is correct.

Bottom Line

Neat, justified, readable mathematics earns more credit than an unexplained answer. This worksheet is designed to reward careful setup, correct use of trigonometry, and professional-quality written work.

Calculator use may be permitted on the worksheet, but calculator use does not replace explanation.
