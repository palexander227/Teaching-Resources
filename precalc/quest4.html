Quest 4: Logarithmic and Exponential Equations

Directions: Solve each problem and enter the requested encoded value.

For rational answers, write \(x=\frac{a}{b}\) in lowest terms with \(b>0\). Enter \(a+b\).

For problems with two solutions, enter the sum of the solutions.

If there is no solution, type no solution.

Check buttons are available only on problems 5, 10, 15, 20, and 25.
