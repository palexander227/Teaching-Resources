Rational Functions / Removable Discontinuity

Problem 1

Let

\[ f(x)=\frac{x^2-36}{x-6}. \]

The function has a value \(k\), omitted from its domain, such that for all \(x\neq k\), \(f(x)\) agrees with a linear function

\[ ax+b. \]

Find

\[ k+a+b. \]
Piecewise Continuity

Problem 2

\[ f(x)=\begin{cases} 5x-4,&x<2\\ ax+3,&x\ge2 \end{cases} \]

The function is continuous at \(x=2\) for exactly one value of \(a\).

If

\[ a=\frac{m}{n} \]

in lowest terms, find

\[ m+n. \]
Logarithm Expansion

Problem 3

Suppose

\[ \log_3(81x^5y^{-2})=a+b\log_3x+c\log_3y. \]

Find

\[ a+b+c. \]
Average Rate of Change

Problem 4

Let

\[ f(x)=4x^2-7x+1. \]

The average rate of change of \(f\) on the interval

\[ [a,a+3] \]

is \(41\).

If

\[ a=\frac{m}{n} \]

in lowest terms, find

\[ m+n. \]
Geometric Series

Problem 5

The infinite geometric series

\[ 18+12+8+\frac{16}{3}+\cdots \]

has sum \(S\). Find \(S\).
