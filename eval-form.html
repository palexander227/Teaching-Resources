Persuasive Pitch Peer Evaluation

Instructions: Evaluate your classmate's 5-minute persuasive pitch using the rubric below.



Category Description Score (1–5)
Clarity of Idea Was the core idea clearly stated and easy to understand?
Use of Pitch Structure Did the presentation follow the structure: "What if you could…", "so that…", "for example…", "but that's not all…"
Persuasiveness How convincing and compelling was the argument?
Examples / Evidence Did the presenter provide clear examples or evidence supporting the idea?
Presentation Skills Eye contact, speaking volume, pacing, engagement with audience.
Slide Quality Slides were clear, readable, and supported the message.
