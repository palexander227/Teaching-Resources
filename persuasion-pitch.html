The 5-Minute Pitch Project

Goal: Design and deliver a short persuasive pitch for an idea, product, or service.

Your presentation will combine two communication tools we studied:

  • The dialog persuasion template
  • The Problem → Solution → Benefit structure

The dialog template captures attention. The structure ensures your message is logical and convincing.

The Required Persuasion Template

What if you could ______ so that ______?

For example…

But that's not all…

Your pitch must clearly include these phrases as part of your explanation.

Pitch Structure

1. The Hook

Begin with the template:

What if you could ______ so that ______?

This introduces the problem and desired outcome.

2. The Example

Next say:

For example…

Use this section to demonstrate how your idea works.

3. Expansion

Then add:

But that's not all…

Explain additional features or advantages of your idea.

4. The Benefit

Finish by explaining:

  • Why the audience should care
  • Who benefits
  • What problem your idea solves

Slide Requirements (Maximum 3 Slides)

Slide 1 — Hook + Problem
  • Include your “What if you could… so that…” statement
  • Explain the problem your idea addresses
Slide 2 — Example + Solution
  • Include the phrase “For example…”
  • Explain how your idea works
  • Include a diagram or image if helpful
Slide 3 — Expansion + Benefits
  • Include the phrase “But that's not all…”
  • Explain additional features or benefits
  • Explain why people should adopt the idea

Time Limit

  • Maximum: 5 minutes
  • Maximum: 3 slides

Keep slides simple. The focus should be on your speaking.

Possible Pitch Ideas

  • a new app
  • a tutoring platform
  • a school improvement idea
  • a study tool
  • a creative invention
  • a student startup

Your idea does not have to be realistic. Creativity is encouraged.

Evaluation Rubric
