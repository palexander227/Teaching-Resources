Survey rank 1 · Whole-student admissions

Institution & Program Evaluation

Evaluate one student–institution–program pairing through separate competitiveness and fit lenses.

1

Survey-derived case

The strongest general-survey priority: contextual competitiveness was selected by 8 of 10 respondents and ranked first by 6; college/program fit was among the highest-rated profile dimensions.

Primary boundary

No admit/reject verdict; no single-point probability; evaluates one pairing rather than constructing the full college list.

Consultant workflow

  1. Select student, institution, program or major, and application round.
  2. Check profile completeness and identify missing or weak evidence.
  3. Define and inspect the comparison cohort.
  4. Review competitiveness and fit through separate lenses.
  5. Inspect comparable cases, source evidence, sample size, and uncertainty.
  6. Record the consultant’s interpretation and recommended next steps.

Inputs

Transcript, course rigor, testing, activities, intended major, essays, school context, historical cases and outcomes.

Outputs

Competitiveness range, fit assessment, strengths and risks, comparable cases, evidence lineage, confidence and consultant notes.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityHigh
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityHigh
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Advance as a bounded pilot for selected institutions and programs.

Survey rank 2 · Whole-student admissions

Comparable Applicant & Profile Benchmarking

Find relevant historical applicants and show how the current student is similar, stronger, weaker, or simply different.

2

Survey-derived case

Two of the three strongest general-survey needs concerned comparative strengths/weaknesses and prior Ilumin applicant patterns; each was selected by 8 of 10 respondents.

Primary boundary

No opaque universal similarity score; historical outcomes inform but do not determine the present case.

Consultant workflow

  1. Choose the comparison purpose and relevant dimensions.
  2. Retrieve candidate historical comparables.
  3. Show why each case was selected.
  4. Compare the student dimension by dimension.
  5. Let the consultant retain, remove, or qualify cases.
  6. Save a consultant-approved benchmark set.

Inputs

Normalized historical profiles, outcomes, intended majors, institution/program context, academic and activity features.

Outputs

Comparable-case set, similarity rationale, relative strengths and risks, historical outcome context and consultant qualifications.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityHigh
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityHigh
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Strong pilot candidate using an audited institutional subset.

Survey rank 3 · Essay development

Essay Concept Strategy Studio

Help consultants establish an essay concept through topic evidence, topic selection, and meaning or reframing.

3

Survey-derived case

Historical topic distribution with outcome context ranked first by 7 of 10 essay respondents; topic recommendation ranked second. Qualitative responses repeatedly described the movement from event to meaning.

Primary boundary

Evidence and suggestions do not replace student agency or consultant judgment; no school recommendation from essay text alone.

Consultant workflow

  1. Identify prompt and essay genre.
  2. Review historical topic patterns and outcome context.
  3. Elicit candidate experiences from the student.
  4. Compare topic options without reducing them to a score.
  5. Clarify what the chosen experience is truly about.
  6. Record the consultant-approved concept and cautions.

Inputs

Prompt, genre, whole-student context, prior essay topics, anonymized exemplars and validated outcomes.

Outputs

Topic landscape, candidate concepts, distinctiveness context, meaning/reframing map and consultant-selected direction.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityHigh
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityMedium
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Highly demonstrable bounded prototype with a curated essay corpus.

Survey rank 4 · Whole-student admissions

Near-Term Action Prioritizer

Identify the highest-value improvements that remain realistically achievable before deadlines.

4

Survey-derived case

Actionable improvements before deadlines ranked fourth overall and were selected by 6 of 10 respondents.

Primary boundary

Recommendations are options, not unilateral prescriptions; only realistic actions enter the final plan.

Consultant workflow

  1. Confirm deadlines and remaining time.
  2. Review strengths, risks, and evidence gaps.
  3. Estimate effort, feasibility, and likely strategic value.
  4. Generate an ordered set of options.
  5. Let the consultant accept, modify, or reject recommendations.
  6. Convert approved actions into a timeline.

Inputs

Student profile, application calendar, evidence gaps, target institutions, major strategy and available resources.

Outputs

Prioritized actions, effort/value tradeoffs, deadlines, dependencies and consultant-approved plan.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityHigh
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityMedium
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Strong pilot candidate once the student profile and deadline model are available.

Survey rank 5 · Whole-student admissions

Academic & Career Direction Explorer

Explore academic fields and career pathways that fit the student, using separate major-direction and career-direction modes.

5

Survey-derived case

Major and career direction was selected by 9 of 10 respondents as an area where analytics would help.

Primary boundary

Exploration does not decide the student’s future; career direction remains a secondary mode with lower direct survey support.

Consultant workflow

  1. Review interests, preparation, activities, values, and opportunity context.
  2. Generate plausible academic directions.
  3. Inspect major alignment and gaps.
  4. Explore related career pathways separately.
  5. Compare alternatives and tradeoffs.
  6. Record the consultant’s directional judgment.

Inputs

Academic record, activities, student interests, reflective materials, intended fields and program information.

Outputs

Academic-direction options, career-pathway options, alignment evidence, gaps and consultant conclusion.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityHigh
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityHigh
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Prototype as a guided exploration rather than an autonomous recommender.

Survey rank 6 · Whole-student admissions

Major Strategy Analyzer

Evaluate how intended or alternate-major choices change competitiveness, school options, and application positioning.

6

Survey-derived case

The effect of intended major on competitiveness and strategy was selected by 6 of 10 respondents and recurred strongly in open-response examples.

Primary boundary

Does not determine what the student should study; that belongs to the Direction Explorer.

Consultant workflow

  1. Select intended and alternate majors.
  2. Compare program structures and selectivity context.
  3. Evaluate profile alignment for each option.
  4. Inspect historical comparable cases.
  5. Surface strategic tradeoffs across schools.
  6. Record the consultant’s major-strategy judgment.

Inputs

Student profile, intended majors, program-level historical data, institution rules and application options.

Outputs

Major-option comparison, competitiveness shifts, alignment evidence, alternate-major considerations and consultant notes.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityHigh
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityHigh
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Pilot only where program- and major-level data are sufficiently complete.

Survey rank 7 · Whole-student admissions

Student Evidence & Readiness Profile

Orient the consultant to the complete case and distinguish missing information from weak evidence.

7

Survey-derived case

A concise student profile and evidence supporting assessments were each selected by 6 of 10 respondents; missing or weak-evidence flags by 5 of 10.

Primary boundary

The profile is not a verdict or a generic dashboard; it is an evidence-oriented case review.

Consultant workflow

  1. Assemble the canonical student record.
  2. Show source documents and provenance.
  3. Summarize profile dimensions without hiding missingness.
  4. Flag absent information and weakly supported claims separately.
  5. Invite consultant corrections and annotations.
  6. Publish an approved case snapshot for downstream tools.

Inputs

CouncilMore fields, Google Drive documents, transcripts, essays, activities, notes and source metadata.

Outputs

Student overview, evidence map, completeness status, weak-evidence flags, provenance and consultant annotations.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityMedium
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityMedium
September feasibilityStrong

Working recommendation: Best foundational vertical slice and likely prerequisite for several other tools.

Survey rank 8 · Whole-student admissions

College-List Builder

Construct a balanced school portfolio using competitiveness, fit, major strategy, and family preferences.

8

Survey-derived case

College-list construction was selected as useful by 6 of 10 respondents and appeared in concrete family and sales scenarios.

Primary boundary

Reach/target/likely labels are outputs, not the tool’s governing logic; the tool never removes options automatically.

Consultant workflow

  1. Capture family preferences and constraints.
  2. Evaluate candidate institutions through fit and competitiveness lenses.
  3. Balance the portfolio across strategic categories.
  4. Explain why each school is included.
  5. Identify list gaps or concentration risks.
  6. Record the consultant-approved list.

Inputs

Student profile, institution evaluations, family preferences, financial and geographic constraints, major strategy.

Outputs

Balanced college list, inclusion rationales, portfolio balance, cautions and consultant-approved categories.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityHigh
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityHigh
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Strong market-facing candidate after the evaluation engine is available.

Survey rank 9 · Whole-student admissions

Application Positioning Planner

Decide what the application should emphasize for a particular institution, program, or major.

9

Survey-derived case

Application positioning was selected by 6 of 10 respondents and remained distinct from major strategy and profile coherence during adjudication.

Primary boundary

Does not manufacture a story or overwrite authenticity; diagnosis and strategy remain inspectable.

Consultant workflow

  1. Review the whole student profile and intended destination.
  2. Assess coherence, major alignment, and program fit.
  3. Identify differentiating evidence.
  4. Select themes to emphasize and material to de-emphasize.
  5. Check activities and essays for alignment.
  6. Record the consultant’s positioning brief.

Inputs

Student evidence profile, institution/program evaluation, activities, essays, major strategy and family context.

Outputs

Positioning thesis, supporting evidence, alignment review, risks and consultant-approved emphasis.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityHigh
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityMedium
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Develop after the profile and institution-evaluation foundations.

Survey rank 10 · Whole-student admissions

Early-Application Strategy Planner

Evaluate where, whether, and how the student should use ED, EA, or other early-round opportunities.

10

Survey-derived case

ED/EA/RD strategy was selected by 5 of 10 respondents, with concrete examples where early-round choices materially affected advice.

Primary boundary

The system supports rather than makes the binding decision; missing round-specific data must be visible.

Consultant workflow

  1. Select candidate early-round institutions.
  2. Review binding rules, fit, competitiveness, and family constraints.
  3. Compare early and regular-round tradeoffs.
  4. Inspect relevant historical cases where available.
  5. Surface strategic risks and opportunity costs.
  6. Record the consultant-supported family decision.

Inputs

College list, institution/program evaluations, round-specific data, family preferences, financial constraints and deadlines.

Outputs

Early-round comparison, strategic tradeoffs, cautions, deadline plan and consultant recommendation.

Provisional delivery assessment

MarketabilityMedium
Demonstration valueHigh
Data readinessPartial
ComplexityMedium
September feasibilityConditional

Working recommendation: Pilot after the college-list and institution-evaluation workflows are connected.
