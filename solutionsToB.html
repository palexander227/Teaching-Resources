Problem 1

Pendulum model

A pendulum is at its highest point of 36 cm at the beginning of the measurement, one second later it is at its lowest point of 12 cm, and one second after that it is back at its highest point.
Key features:
  • Maximum height: \(36\)
  • Minimum height: \(12\)
  • Amplitude: \(\dfrac{36-12}{2} = 12\)
  • Midline: \(\dfrac{36+12}{2} = 24\)
  • From max to min takes 1 second, so that is half a period.
  • Therefore the period is \(2\) seconds.

(a) Sketch information

A correct sketch starts at a maximum at \((0,36)\), reaches a minimum at \((1,12)\), and returns to a maximum at \((2,36)\). The sinusoid oscillates about the midline \(y=24\).

(b) One suitable equation

Since the graph starts at a maximum, cosine is the natural choice: \[ h(t)=12\cos(\pi t)+24. \]

Here \(B=\pi\) because \(\dfrac{2\pi}{B}=2\).

Problem 2

Polynomial analysis

Analyze \(f(x)=x^5+5x^4+7x^3-x^2-8x-4\): maximum possible number of turns, real zeros and multiplicities, x-intercepts where the graph crosses or does not cross, y-intercept, end behavior, and a sketch description.

1. Maximum possible number of turns

The polynomial has degree \(5\), so the maximum possible number of turning points is \[ 5-1=4. \]

2. Factor completely

Using the hint that \((x-1)\) and \((x+2)\) are factors is helpful, but the complete factorization is \[ f(x)=x^5+5x^4+7x^3-x^2-8x-4=(x-1)(x+1)^2(x+2)^2. \]

3. Real zeros and multiplicities

\[ x=1 \text{ with multiplicity }1, \qquad x=-1 \text{ with multiplicity }2, \qquad x=-2 \text{ with multiplicity }2. \]

4. Crossing behavior at the x-axis

  • At \(x=1\), the multiplicity is odd, so the graph crosses the x-axis.
  • At \(x=-1\), the multiplicity is even, so the graph touches and turns.
  • At \(x=-2\), the multiplicity is even, so the graph touches and turns.

5. y-intercept

\[ f(0)=-4. \] So the y-intercept is \((0,-4)\).

6. End behavior

The leading term is \(x^5\), which has odd degree and positive leading coefficient, so: \[ x\to -\infty \Rightarrow f(x)\to -\infty, \qquad x\to \infty \Rightarrow f(x)\to \infty. \] Left end down, right end up.

7. Sketch description

Start far down on the left. The graph rises to touch the x-axis at \(x=-2\), turns back, then rises again to touch the x-axis at \(x=-1\), turns back again, passes through \((0,-4)\), and then crosses the x-axis at \(x=1\) before rising to the right.
Problem 3

Solve the triangle

The three side lengths are \(AB=17\), \(BC=15\), and \(AC=28\). Find all angles.
Let \[ a=BC=15, \qquad b=AC=28, \qquad c=AB=17, \] so that \(a\) is opposite \(\angle A\), \(b\) is opposite \(\angle B\), and \(c\) is opposite \(\angle C\).

Find \(\angle A\)

By the Law of Cosines, \[ a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A. \] Hence \[ 15^2=28^2+17^2-2(28)(17)\cos A. \] So \[ \cos A=\frac{28^2+17^2-15^2}{2\cdot 28\cdot 17} =\frac{848}{952}\approx 0.890756, \] and therefore \[ A\approx 27.032^\circ. \]

Find \(\angle B\)

\[ b^2=a^2+c^2-2ac\cos B \] gives \[ 28^2=15^2+17^2-2(15)(17)\cos B. \] Thus \[ \cos B=\frac{15^2+17^2-28^2}{2\cdot 15\cdot 17} =\frac{-270}{510}\approx -0.529412, \] so \[ B\approx 121.966^\circ. \]

Find \(\angle C\)

\[ C=180^\circ-A-B \approx 180^\circ-27.032^\circ-121.966^\circ \approx 31.003^\circ. \]
Final answer: \[ \angle A\approx 27.032^\circ,\qquad \angle B\approx 121.966^\circ,\qquad \angle C\approx 31.003^\circ. \]
Problem 4

Seasonal computer sales model

The model is \[ s(t)=0.098\sin(0.79t+2.37)+0.387, \qquad 1\le t\le 12, \] where \(t\) is time in quarters and \(s(t)\) is quarterly revenue in millions of dollars.

Part A

(a) Amplitude

The amplitude is \[ 0.098. \] Interpretation: the revenue fluctuates by about \(0.098\) million dollars above and below the midline. That is about \(\$98{,}000\).

(b) Period

\[ P=\frac{2\pi}{0.79}\approx 7.953. \] Interpretation: one full sales cycle takes about 7.953 quarters. This means each revenue cycle starts down with three quarters before the first increments.

(c) Vertical shift

The vertical shift is \[ 0.387. \] Interpretation: the midline is \(s=0.387\), so the average quarterly revenue is about \(0.387\) million dollars, or \(\$387{,}000\).

(d) Phase shift

Rewrite conceptually as \(A\sin(B(t-h))+D\), where \[ h=-\frac{2.37}{0.79}=-3. \] So the phase shift is 3 quarters left.

Part B

Useful graph features

  • Midline: \(y=0.387\)
  • Maximum value: \(0.387+0.098=0.485\)
  • Minimum value: \(0.387-0.098=0.289\)
  • At \(t=0\): \(s(0)=0.098\sin(2.37)+0.387\approx 0.455\)

(a) How long until maximum revenue is attained?

Maximum occurs when \[ \sin(0.79t+2.37)=1. \] So \[ 0.79t+2.37=\frac{\pi}{2}+2\pi k. \] The first solution in the interval \(1\le t\le 12\) is \[ t\approx 6.942. \] So maximum revenue is attained at about 6.942 quarters.

(b) How long until \(s(0)\) is again observed?

We solve \[ 0.098\sin(0.79t+2.37)+0.387=s(0). \] This means \[ \sin(0.79t+2.37)=\sin(2.37). \] The first positive solution after \(t=0\) is \[ t\approx 5.930. \] So \(s(0)\) is again observed after about 5.930 quarters.
